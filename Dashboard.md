## 📊 Power BI Dashboard

The dashboard presents Gold-layer aggregates from the fraud detection pipeline,
built for audit reporting and day-to-day fraud monitoring across 500,000+
synthetic transactions.

**What it shows:**
- **Fraud classification breakdown** — Normal, Negative Balance, Extreme Amount, and High Amount categories
- **Daily transaction volume and value** — total transactions and average transaction amount per day
- **Suspicious vs. invalid transaction rates** — flagged fraud percentage alongside data-quality issues like missing timestamps or invalid users
- **Top-risk accounts** — accounts with the highest concentration of flagged activity, for audit follow-up

*Data is synthetically generated to simulate a high-volume banking transaction environment for pipeline stress-testing.*
